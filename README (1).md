# Coffee_Sales_Analysis
PROBLEM STATEMENT

***KPI's REQUIREMENT***:

We need to analyze key indicators for our Coffee sales data to gain insights into our business performance. Specifically, we want to calculate the following metrics:
1.Total Revenue: The sum of the total price of all Coffee orders
2.Average Order Value: The average amount spent per order, calculated by dividing the total revenue by the total number of orders.
3.Total Coffees Sold: The sum of the quantities of all Coffees sold.
4.Total Orders: The total number of orders placed.
5.Average Coffees Per Order: The average number of Coffees sold per order, calculated by dividing the total number of Coffees sold by the total number of orders.

***CHARTS REQUIREMENT***:

We would like to visualize various aspects of our Coffee sales data to gain insights and understand key trends. We have identified the following requirements for creating charts:

1.Daily Trend for Total Orders:
Create a bar chart that displays the daily trend of total orders over a specific time period. This chart willchelp us identify any patterns or fluctuations in order volumes on a daily basis.

2.Monthly Trend for Total Orders:
Create a line chart that illustrates the hourly trend of total orders throughout the day. This chart will allowus to identify peak hours or periods of high order activity.

3.Percentage of Sales by Coffee Category:
Create a pie chart that shows the distribution of sales across different Coffee categories. This chart will provide insights into the popularity of various Coffee categories and their contribution to overall sales.

4.Percentage of Sales by Coffee Size:
Generate a pie chart that represents the percentage of sales attributed to different Coffee sizes. This chart will help us understand customer preferences for Coffee sizes and their impact on sales.

5.Total Coffees Sold by Coffee Category:
Create a funnel chart that presents the total number of Coffees sold for each Coffee category. This chart will allow us to compare the sales performance of different Coffee categories.

6.Top 5 Best Sellers by Revenue, Total Quantity and Total Orders
Create a bar chart highlighting the top 5 best-selling Coffees based on the Revenue, Total Quantity, Total  Orders. This chart will help us identify the most popular Coffee options.

7.Bottom 5 Best Sellers by Revenue, Total Quantity and Total Orders
Create a bar chart showcasing the bottom 5 worst-selling Coffees based on the Revenue, Total Quantity, Total Orders. This chart will enable us to identify underperforming or less popular Coffee options.
